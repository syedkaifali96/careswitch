# Optional future live head-control recording

The current [published presentation](https://youtu.be/PYqF6pDfuLU) is complete as an edited app-capture video and contains no live head-control recording. This guide is for an **optional future revision**, not a statement that such footage exists or an extra verified organizer requirement.

If the creator chooses to record, first review the actual camera behavior comfortably on their own device. Do not record a patient or another person's face without appropriate consent. Do not portray a healthy demonstration as a clinical/intended-user trial. The agent has not opened the creator's real camera for this task.

1. Open the public demo and close unrelated tabs/notifications. Avoid personal information in custom phrases.
2. Enable the camera and follow the five comfortable calibration prompts. Record only if willing to share the visible webcam preview; obtain help if needed.
3. Capture a continuous segment showing the actual in-app pointer moving to a card, a completed hold and a silent draft.
4. Move into neutral space, then hold on Cancel once. Repeat a card selection and hold on Confirm.
5. Show confirmed text and any actual speech outcome. If audio is blocked, show the real status and Read again; do not edit a failure into a fabricated pass.
6. Include a short limitation statement. Stop or pause if movements are uncomfortable. Review the full recording for personal content before sharing it.

Label the clip with its real input mode and device. It can supplement a presentation, but its existence alone would not establish clinical safety, fatigue tolerance or suitability for intended users. No recording or replacement upload is authorized merely by this guide.
