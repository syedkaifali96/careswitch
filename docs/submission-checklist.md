## Honest testing statement

The creator reported that live camera/head control worked on their laptop after calibration and pointer adjustments. Automated tests cover calibration, dwell, pointer stability, camera startup and speech cancellation. Browser tests used the real landmark model on public sample-image streams, and the real speech engine completed playback for all eight message language choices. These sample-stream checks are not intended-user testing. Latest free-voice pronunciation/listening on the creator's physical device, physical phone support, fatigue and clinical usefulness remain to be checked.

## Do not claim

No diagnosis, treatment, emergency alert, caregiver contact, emotion or pain detection, eye-gaze tracking, clinical validation, every-language support, universal phone support, natural neural voice, or world's-first invention. Surrounding setup UI is English. Voice engine is rule-based; face landmarks are the AI component. A future benefit is a hypothesis until tested.
